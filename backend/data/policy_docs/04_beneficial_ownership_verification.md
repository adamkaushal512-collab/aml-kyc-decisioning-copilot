# Policy AML-038: Beneficial Ownership Verification Requirements

**Scope:** Applies to all legal entity customers at onboarding and at each
periodic KYC refresh.

## Clauses

1. For any legal entity customer, the institution must identify and verify
   the identity of each beneficial owner who owns, directly or indirectly,
   25% or more of the entity's equity interests.

2. If no individual meets the 25% ownership threshold, the institution must
   identify and verify at least one individual with significant managerial
   control over the entity (a "control person").

3. If the entity's ownership structure includes intermediate holding
   companies, trusts, or nominee arrangements, the institution must trace
   ownership through each layer until a natural person is identified at the
   top of the chain; verification is not complete at the level of another
   legal entity.

4. If beneficial ownership cannot be fully verified despite reasonable
   efforts, the case must be escalated for a risk-based decision on whether
   to proceed, restrict, or decline the relationship.

5. Beneficial ownership information must be re-verified at every periodic
   KYC refresh and immediately upon notice of a material change in
   ownership or control.

6. If a beneficial owner identified during verification also returns a
   sanctions or PEP match (per Policies AML-014 and AML-021), that match
   must be handled under the applicable escalation requirements as if the
   beneficial owner were the primary customer.

7. All beneficial ownership determinations, including the ownership chain
   documentation, must be retained in the case's audit record.
