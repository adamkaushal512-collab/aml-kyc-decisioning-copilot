# Policy AML-021: Politically Exposed Person (PEP) Handling Requirements

**Scope:** Applies to all customers, beneficial owners, and authorized
signatories identified as a Politically Exposed Person (PEP), a PEP family
member, or a PEP close associate.

## Clauses

1. If a customer or beneficial owner is identified as a foreign PEP, the
   case must be escalated to Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) regardless of the
   screening similarity score that identified them.

2. If a customer is identified as a domestic PEP, the case requires
   Enhanced Due Diligence only when combined with one or more additional
   risk factors (e.g., high-risk jurisdiction, cash-intensive business, or
   an unusually large transaction relative to the customer's known profile).

3. Enhanced Due Diligence for a confirmed PEP must include: (a) verification
   of the source of wealth and source of funds, (b) senior management
   approval before the relationship is onboarded or continued, and (c)
   ongoing monitoring at a heightened frequency, no less than annually.

4. If a PEP relationship's source of wealth cannot be reasonably verified,
   the case must be escalated for a decision on whether to decline or exit
   the relationship; the case may not be cleared solely on the customer's
   self-attestation.

5. PEP status must be reassessed at every periodic KYC refresh, as an
   individual's PEP classification can change (e.g., leaving public office
   does not immediately remove PEP status; a cooling-off period applies).

6. All PEP determinations and their supporting rationale must be recorded
   in the case's audit trail.
